Questions about trading

How do I exchange Trade Tokens in Pokemon TCG Pocket?

Trade Tokens no longer exist: Shinedust replaced them in July 2025, and the tokens the players had were turned into Shinedust. Trades are now paid in Shinedust.

How do I get Shinedust?

You get Shinedust when you obtain a card you already have, and from missions and events.

Which cards cannot be traded?

Cards with three stars (immersive), crown cards and promo cards cannot be traded. The site shows them with 🚫, and leaves them out of the proposals.

Can I trade with any player online?

Only with your friends in the game. When the site finds you a match, add the other player with their Friend ID or their QR code, then start the trade in the game.
